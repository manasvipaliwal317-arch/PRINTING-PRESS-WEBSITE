Who We Are

Mastering the Art & Engineering of Commercial Printing

PrintCraft Pro is a leading full-service commercial printing press dedicated to serving corporate brands, marketing agencies, retail businesses, and individual creators. We operate a state-of-the-art 35,000 sq ft production facility equipped with high-speed 6-color Heidelberg offset presses, liquid ink HP Indigo digital presses, and automated finishing lines.

From initial prepress digital inspection to final die-cutting and shipping, our team guarantees zero compromise on color accuracy, paper tactile quality, and delivery schedules.

Our Mission

To empower businesses of every scale with premium, vibrant, and reliable print solutions. We bridge traditional press craftsmanship with rapid digital technology to make high-end commercial printing accessible, affordable, and eco-friendly.

Our Vision

To be the world’s most trusted commercial printing press standard, recognized for pioneering sustainable soy-based inks, automated 24-hour prepress workflows, and innovative custom packaging finishes.

25 Years of Excellence

Our Heritage & Story

How a small letterpress shop grew into an industry leader in commercial printing engineering.

Founded in 1998 in New York's industrial district, PrintCraft Pro began as a modest letterpress shop with two manual presses and a passion for crisp typography. As commercial print requirements evolved, we invested early in multi-color Heidelberg offset presses and computerized ink density management.

Over the past two decades, we expanded our facility to 35,000 sq ft, adding high-speed liquid ink digital production lines, large-format UV roll printers, and custom packaging structural die-cutting machinery. Today, we handle over 50,000 print runs annually for clients ranging from local artisan brands to Fortune 500 enterprises.
