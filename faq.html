Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Search or click any question below to view details on turnarounds, custom orders, file formats, and delivery.

What is your turnaround time?

Our standard production turnaround is 3 to 4 business days. For urgent deadlines, we offer 24-Hour Express Production and Same-Day Emergency Dispatch for orders submitted before 10:00 AM EST with approved prepress digital proofing.

Do you offer custom printing?

Yes! We specialize in custom commercial printing tailored to your exact specifications. This includes custom paper stock GSMs, custom die-cut structural packaging boxes, metallic gold/silver foil stamping, raised spot UV gloss, embossing, and non-standard print dimensions.

What file formats do you accept?

We recommend print-ready PDF files (PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 preset). We also accept Adobe Illustrator (.AI), Photoshop (.PSD), InDesign (.INDD), EPS, TIFF, and high-res PNG/JPG files. All raster images should be 300 DPI with CMYK color profiles and 3mm (0.125") bleeds included.

Can I order in bulk?

Absolutely. We operate high-speed Heidelberg 6-color offset presses designed specifically for large volume production runs. Bulk volume discounts are automatically applied for orders of 500, 1,000, 5,000, and 50,000+ units.

Do you provide delivery?

Yes, we provide nationwide ground and expedited courier delivery. Standard ground shipping is free on qualifying orders over $150. Same-day local messenger delivery is also available across the New York metro area.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Wire Transfer / ACH payments, and corporate purchase orders (P.O.) for approved commercial accounts.
